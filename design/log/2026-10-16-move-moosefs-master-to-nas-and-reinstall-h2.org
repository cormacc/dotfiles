* Intent
Free the Odroid H2+ of its MooseFS master, Plex and Roon roles and reinstall it with NixOS as a storage-only node, by moving those services to the NixOS NAS, without data loss in a MooseFS cluster that is about 90% full.

* Summary
*Level:* production · *Tests:* smoke · *Docs:* inline

Draft. The target host responsibilities, the cluster figures and the rejected alternatives are in =design/home-network-infrastructure.org= (Host responsibilities). On 2026-10-16 the H2+ (=mfsmaster=, 192.168.1.195, Ubuntu focal) ran the MooseFS master 4.58.3, a chunkserver on port 9460 with two 10 TB disks (=/mnt/chunks/10t01=, =/mnt/chunks/10t02=; 311,271 chunks; labels =B,H=), Plex and Roon. The other chunkservers and the three metaloggers (on the HC4s) ran 4.59.2, which matches nixpkgs =moosefs=.

** Scope
*** In scope
- Inventory of the H2+: MooseFS configuration, disk UUIDs, Plex and Roon installation and data locations.
- MooseFS master and CGI monitor on the NAS (=services.moosefs.master=, =services.moosefs.cgiserver=), with the =mfsmaster= DNS record moved to the NAS.
- Plex on the NAS (=services.plex=, with =accelerationDevices= for =/dev/dri=).
- Roon Server on the NAS (=services.roon-server=).
- NixOS reinstall of the H2+ in place: a new =x86_64-linux= host in =flake.nix= with =services.moosefs.chunkserver= on the existing disks, and =services.moosefs.metalogger=.
- Relevant contract: =design/home-network-infrastructure.org= (Host responsibilities).
*** Out of scope
- OS changes on the HC4 and HC2 nodes, added capacity, HC2 retirement, the DGX Spark migration.
- The edge Raspberry Pi 4 (task 2d737e59).
- Changes to storage classes.

*** Edge cases
- [ ] The master move can be rolled back until the old master is decommissioned; the steps are written down before the cutover.
- [ ] No re-replication starts while the H2+ chunkserver is offline for the reinstall.
*** Anti-criteria
- [ ] Must not: format or repartition the H2+ chunk disks.
- [ ] Must not: run two MooseFS masters at the same time.
- [ ] Must not: start the H2+ reinstall while any chunk is under goal.

** Decisions
- Cut over the master by a clean stop and a copy of =metadata.mfs= :: a clean stop writes complete metadata, so the new master starts from it directly.
  Rejected:
  - Promote metalogger data with =mfsmaster -a=: intended for a lost master; a user report (moosefs/moosefs#278) describes lost changes after an unclean recovery.
- Reinstall the H2+ in place, keeping the chunk disks :: the cluster cannot absorb its 18.1 TB.
  Rejected:
  - Drain the H2+ first: about 14.1 TB free across the cluster on 2026-10-16.

** Risks
- Single-copy window :: while the H2+ is offline, its chunks have one copy elsewhere; a second disk failure in that window loses data. The HC2 disk showed errors (last 2026-10-08). Check the HC2 first and keep the window short.
- Cached master address :: chunkservers, metaloggers or clients may keep the old address after the DNS change; restart or remount them.
- Plex or Roon metadata loss :: libraries reference media paths; keep the same paths on the NAS.
- Roon licence :: a licence covers one Roon core; release the old core when the NAS core signs in.
